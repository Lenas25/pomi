# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link and applies to every space where the project is discussed (issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the maintainers).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, give and accept feedback with respect, and do not harass or discriminate against anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **[easp0104@gmail.com](mailto:easp0104@gmail.com)**. Reports are handled privately and in good faith. The enforcement guidelines of the Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) apply.
